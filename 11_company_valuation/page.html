4. What the share price assumes

A valuation run backwards. Keep your other settings, and ask what one assumption would have to be for the model to agree with the market.

Not advice. This page shows a method on public data. It does not say whether anyone should buy or sell the shares. A model this simple leaves out things the market may be paying for, and its answer swings widely with its assumptions.

Sources: . Free cash flow here is operating profit after tax, plus depreciation, less spending on buildings and equipment, less the change in working capital. Leased shops are treated as a running cost, not as debt.
